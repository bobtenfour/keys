# Product Vision

## Authority
This document is the highest product authority for KeyInventory.

## Purpose
Define what KeyInventory is, who it serves, what problems it solves, and what it must never become.

## Vision
KeyInventory is an enterprise-grade physical key inventory and custody platform for organizations that require controlled issuance, traceable possession, immutable auditability, policy-driven security, and operational accountability.

## Target Users
- Security operations
- Facilities management
- Operations management
- Authorized employees
- Contractors under controlled workflows
- Auditors
- System administrators

## Product Outcomes
KeyInventory must:
- Control physical key cataloging.
- Control key issuance and return.
- Preserve full custody traceability.
- Prevent orphan custody records.
- Provide immutable audit evidence.
- Support future policy-driven authorization.
- Support future multi-site operations without redesign.
- Support future smart cabinet integration without redesign.
- Support future analytics and reporting without replacing transactional authority.
- Remain enterprise-ready from the foundation.

## Product Non-Goals
KeyInventory is not:
- A building access control system.
- A badge management system.
- A locksmith ERP.
- A generic asset inventory system.
- A visitor management system.
- A patient, HR, payroll, or identity master system.

## Boundary
This document never defines implementation, files, projects, database schema, UI screens, runtime workflow details, or slice sequencing.
